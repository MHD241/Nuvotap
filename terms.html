Cancelling an order

To cancel an order, email hello@nuvotap.com as soon as you can with your name and order details. We reply as soon as we can on working days.

We try to prepare and send orders quickly, so we may not always be able to stop an order in time. If your order has already been sent, you can still return it under our returns rules below.

If we receive your cancellation before your order is sent, we will cancel it and refund the full amount using your original payment method.

Returns and refunds

Change of mind. If you bought as a consumer online or by email, you can tell us within 14 days of receiving your items that you want to cancel. You then have a further 14 days to send them back. You do not need to give a reason. You pay the return postage for change-of-mind returns. We recommend a tracked service.

Programmed and personalised items. A standard card is not excluded from change-of-mind returns just because we programmed your link onto it. Items made specially to your own design may be excluded. If so, we will tell you before we start work. Digital profiles are a service and may follow different cancellation rules. We will explain these and ask for your agreement before we start work.

Faulty or wrong items. If an item is faulty, damaged, not as described, or not what you ordered, contact us. We will put it right and pay reasonable return costs. If we made a mistake with programming, we will fix it free of charge.

How to return. Email hello@nuvotap.com with your name, order details and the item. Wait for us to send you the return address before you post anything.

Your refund. We refund to your original payment method. We normally do this within 14 days of receiving the item back, or of you showing us that you posted it, whichever comes first. For change-of-mind cancellations, we refund the item price and our standard delivery charge, but not the cost of sending the item back to us.

30-Day Review Guarantee. On Google Review Cards, we also offer our own extra guarantee, on top of your legal rights. Follow our guide for 30 days, and if you get fewer than 10 new genuine Google reviews, we take the card back and refund the card price. Full conditions are in our Review Guide and Returns & Refunds Policy.
