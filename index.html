03 / FAQ
What exactly does an NFC review card do?+

It contains a chip that opens your business's configured Google review page on a compatible smartphone. It doesn't write or submit a review for the customer.

Will it work on every phone?+

No. Most newer smartphones support NFC reading, but support, positioning and settings vary. We recommend testing on your customers' common phone types. A printed QR fallback may be offered once production is finalised.

Is there a monthly subscription?+

No. These are proposed one-time purchase products, with no mandatory NUVOTAP subscription.

Can you configure it for my business?+

That's the plan: we'll request your correct review link, programme the chip and test it before delivery.

Can it be reprogrammed?+

Some NFC tags can be rewritten, unless locked after programming. We'll confirm the precise chip and reprogramming policy before accepting live orders.

How long does delivery take and what if it doesn't work?+

Delivery timings, returns and replacement terms will be published before we begin taking payments. You can contact us with questions in the meantime.
