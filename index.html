GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does it work on every phone?

No. NFC compatibility and settings vary by phone. The tag should be tested with compatible devices. Older phones may require extra steps.

Does a tap automatically leave a five-star review?

No. The tag opens the business's Google review link, where the customer independently chooses whether and what to review. Positive reviews are never guaranteed or incentivised.

Can I buy more than one?

Yes. The discount is automatic: one tag costs £20; 2 tags get 10% off, 3 get 15% off, and 4–10 get 20% off. For 11 or more, please email hello@nuvotap.com for a custom quote.

Are these photos of finished NUVOTAP products?

Not yet. The basket works, and the order-enquiry page prepares your details; card payments are not enabled. We'll confirm any order and payment arrangements separately.
