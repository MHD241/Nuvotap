GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

What does an NFC card do?

A compatible smartphone reads the stored data and can open a website, review link or digital profile. It does not create or submit a review automatically.

Can I get an unprogrammed card?

Yes. Blank NFC cards can be ordered without link programming or digital profile setup.

Can the card be locked?

Compatible chips support permanent write protection after testing. Locked NFC memory cannot be rewritten; unlocked cards can usually be reprogrammed.

How much is programming?

Optional URL programming costs £0.50 per physical card. A new digital profile costs £5 per newly created profile.

How do I place an order?

Configure your cards and prepare an email enquiry. We confirm availability, final costs, delivery and payment arrangements separately.
