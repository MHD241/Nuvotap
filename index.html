GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose white or black?

Yes. The current website shows both colour options. Final stock and exact finish will be confirmed at order stage.

How do the discounts work?

Automatic discounts apply by quantity: 2 tags get 10% off, 3 tags get 15% off, and 4 to 10 tags get 20% off. The discount code WELCOME10 can apply an extra 10% off online when valid.

Can I order more than 10?

Yes, but 11 or more tags are treated as a custom order. The website sends you to hello@nuvotap.com for a quote.

Does it automatically leave a review?

No. The tag only opens the Google review link. The customer independently chooses whether to leave feedback.

Can I pay online right now?

The site currently prepares an order enquiry and checkout summary, but secure online card payments are not connected yet.
