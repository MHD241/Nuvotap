QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Good to know.

What does an NFC card do?

A compatible phone reads the NFC link when tapped close to the card. It can open a review page, website or digital profile. It doesn't automatically submit a Google review.

Can I order cards without programming?

Yes. Blank NFC cards can be ordered without programming or profile creation.

What's the difference between locked and unlocked?

Unlocked cards can generally be rewritten. Permanently locked NFC chips cannot be rewritten, even by us. We test cards before locking them. A webpage that the locked URL leads to can still be edited separately.

How does profile setup work?

We manually create each requested profile. A profile needs a name, at least one social link, and either an email address or phone number. Hosting and future edit arrangements are confirmed before you pay.

Can I pay online now?

Not yet. The current site prepares an enquiry that you send via your email app. Orders are only confirmed after we reply and agree final costs.
