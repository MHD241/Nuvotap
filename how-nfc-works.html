See what happens when you tap.

  1. Hold your phone near the card

    No app to open. Just bring the back of the phone close.

  2. The phone powers the card

    The phone gives off a small radio field. The card has a tiny chip and coil that borrow that energy for a moment.

  3. The phone reads the link

    The chip holds a web address. The phone reads it and shows a prompt.

  4. The page opens

    You choose to open it and land on the page the card was set up for, such as a review page.

GOOD TO KNOW

The short version.

  • A few centimetres

    The phone has to be close to the card, usually within about 4 cm.

  • Most modern phones

    Recent iPhones and most Android phones can read a card without an app. Android phones need NFC switched on.

  • Nothing happens by itself

    A tap opens a page. It never writes or sends a review for anyone.
