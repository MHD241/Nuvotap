30-DAY REVIEW GUARANTEE

Your step-by-step review guide

Follow these steps for 30 days. Keep your log. If you have fewer than 10 new genuine Google reviews at the end, we take the card back and refund the price you paid for it.

Start here

Most happy customers will leave a Google review if you ask them at the right moment, and your NUVOTAP card turns that ask into a five-second tap. This guide gives you the steps, the words to say and the proof to keep.

  1. Step 1: Set up your profile and card (about 30 minutes, once).
  2. Step 2: Ask every customer, every day.
  3. Step 3: Ask in a way that brings detailed, honest reviews.
  4. Step 4: Reply to every review.

Step 1: Set up your profile and card

Set up takes about 30 minutes, and the numbers you note on day 1 become your baseline for the guarantee.

  1. Find your business on Google Maps. If it says "Claim this business", claim it. Check your name, opening hours, phone number, category and photos.
  2. Tap your NUVOTAP card with your own phone. It must open your Google review page, not your website. Test it on an iPhone and an Android phone if you can.
  3. Write down your starting numbers: total reviews, average rating and the date of your newest review. Take a dated screenshot of your profile.
  4. Put the card where customers finish: next to the till, or in a stand at eye level. Photograph it in place with the date visible.
  5. Save your review link in your phone notes so you can text it to anyone who cannot tap.
  6. Tell everyone who serves customers to ask the same way (see Step 2).

Step 2: Ask every customer

Ask every customer, not only the ones who seem happy: it is what Google's rules require, and it is what produces a steady flow of reviews. Most satisfied customers never think to leave one unless asked.

Ask at the best moment, straight after the job is done, the payment is taken or the customer says thank you. Say one short sentence, then hand over the card or point to it.

Step 3: Get better reviews

Better reviews come from a well-timed, specific ask made to everyone, never from choosing who gets to review.

  • Ask for detail. Add the same line for every customer: "If you can, mention what we did and what you liked." Detailed reviews read as more trustworthy and help new customers decide.
  • Ask while it is fresh. A review requested at the counter or the same day is longer and more accurate than one requested a week later.
  • Fix problems on the spot. If a customer complains, put it right there and then. Still offer the card the same way as you would to anyone, and let them decide.
  • Invite photos. Customers can add photos to their review. A photo of the finished work or product makes a review stand out.
  • Never ask for five stars. Ask for honest feedback. Never steer, never offer anything in return.

Step 4: Reply to every review

Reply to every review within 3 days: it shows new customers that you listen, and it encourages more people to write one.

  • Positive review: thank them by name, mention one detail they wrote, and invite them back. Example: "Thank you, Sarah. We're glad the cut was just what you wanted. See you next time!"
  • Negative review: stay calm and polite, and never argue in public. Say sorry that the experience fell short, say what you will do, and invite them to contact you directly. Do not share any personal details.
  • After you have put it right: you may ask kindly whether they would consider updating their review. Do not offer anything in return.
  • Fake or abusive reviews: use the three-dot menu on the review and choose "Flag as inappropriate". Do not reply with threats.

The 30-day guarantee

If you follow every step below for 30 days, with proof, and you have fewer than 10 new genuine Google reviews at the end, we take the card back and refund the price you paid for it in full, free of any charge.

You must have done all of these, shown by your log and photos:

  1. Set up and tested the card within 3 days of receiving it, and recorded your baseline.
  2. Asked at least 9 out of every 10 customers for a review, every day, using the card or a text link.
  3. Replied to every review within 3 days.
  4. Kept the proof log for the full 30 days and taken the day 1 and day 30 screenshots.

How to claim:

  1. Within 14 days after day 30, email hello@nuvotap.com with your log and photos.
  2. We check them and confirm in writing. We collect the card from you in Fife, or pay the return postage.
  3. Once we have the card in working condition, we refund you in full within 14 days.

What does not count as a new review: reviews written by you, your staff, family or friends; any review that was paid for or rewarded; and any review Google removes.

Why the guarantee is about review numbers, not stars: nobody honest can promise what customers will write. We guarantee the part you control, which is asking properly and often.

This guarantee is extra. It does not affect your legal rights as a customer. See our Returns & Refunds Policy.

What never to do

Google can freeze new reviews, unpublish existing ones or put a public warning on your profile if you break its review rules.

  • Do not offer a discount, gift or anything else in return for a review.
  • Do not ask only the happy customers, and do not discourage anyone from leaving a negative review.
  • Do not write reviews yourself, or have staff, family or friends write them.
  • Do not ask customers for five stars, or write the review for them.
  • Do not pay anyone for reviews.

Rules can change, so check Google's current policy before you start. NUVOTAP is not affiliated with Google, and a tap never posts a review by itself.
