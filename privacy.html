Privacy & ordering

How enquiries work

NUVOTAP currently uses email-based order enquiries. Adding items to the basket does not place an order, and no card payments are processed on this website. Your email application must be used to send an enquiry. We confirm product availability, delivery charges, payment arrangements, and any profile hosting terms before accepting an order.

Your information

Details entered into the checkout form are used locally in your browser to prepare an email enquiry. The website does not transmit form contents to a NUVOTAP server. If you choose to send the email, the recipient receives the information included in it.

Local browser storage

The basket, theme preference and a discount code are stored in your browser so you can return to your selections. You can clear them using browser site-data settings.

Contact

For ordering or information requests, email hello@nuvotap.com.

A full commercial privacy policy, delivery and returns terms must be completed before live sales.
