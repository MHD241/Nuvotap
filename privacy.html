DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Updated 15 October 2026 · For review before accepting paid orders

NUVOTAP can be contacted at hello@nuvotap.com. The identity and business address of the legal operator must be added before paid sales begin. We aim to explain how customer information is handled clearly.

Information we collect

When you send an order enquiry, we may receive your name, email, phone number, business name, delivery address, order options and any information you choose to include. A Studio design may include your displayed name, business description, social links, contact information, branding colours and photographs or logos.

How we use your information

We use enquiries to respond, prepare quotations, arrange fulfilment and provide customer support. If you subsequently agree to publish a business profile, the content you approve will be visible on the public internet. We will not publish your delivery address merely because you submit an order.

Legal grounds

We generally process enquiry and order details to take steps at your request before entering a contract or to perform an agreed contract. We may also retain records for legal obligations and handle proportionate customer support under legitimate interests where applicable. Any optional marketing will use an appropriate lawful basis and required consent.

How the current website works

Studio designs and baskets may be stored locally in your browser. Where the secure submission feature is connected and you choose Submit, your design details and optional photograph are sent to our configured Supabase backend for private staff review. Photos are held in a private storage bucket and are not public by default. If secure submission is not connected, you may prepare an email enquiry yourself instead. No payment is collected through the Studio.

Sharing, storage and retention

Messages and business records may be handled by our email, hosting and fulfilment service providers where necessary. Supabase is the intended database and private image-storage provider. Its project region, any subprocessors, applicable international transfers and a detailed retention schedule must be confirmed before launch. We should keep personal information only as long as needed for the disclosed purposes or applicable legal obligations and then delete it securely.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, or object to certain uses of your information. Contact hello@nuvotap.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies and browser data

The current website uses local browser storage for shopping and Studio functionality; it does not intentionally install advertising or analytics cookies. We will update this notice and seek consent when required before adding non-essential tracking technologies.

Before launch we must add the legal operator's full identity/address and actual data retention and third-party processing details. This page must be updated whenever the services change.
