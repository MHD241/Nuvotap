Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NUVOTAP uses and protects the information you give us when you use nuvotap.com, order from us or buy from us in person. We take your privacy seriously. If we ask for information that identifies you, we will only use it as this policy describes. We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last changed.

Who we are. NUVOTAP is a trading name of Mohammed Shnina, Dunfermline, Fife, Scotland. We are responsible for your personal information (the "data controller"). For any privacy question or request, email hello@nuvotap.com.

On this page: What we collect · What we do with your information · Who we share it with · How long we keep it · Security · Cookies and browser storage · Links to other websites · Controlling your personal information · List of browser storage we use

What we collect

We may collect:

  • your name and, if you are buying for a business, the business name
  • contact details: email address and, if you choose to give it, a phone number
  • your delivery address
  • your order details: products, options, the web link you want on your cards, and any notes you add
  • if you create an account: your email address, a securely protected password, the details you choose to save, and your daily review log
  • if you use our Studio: the profile details you enter, such as your name, business description, social links, contact details, colours, logos and photographs
  • if you claim our 30-Day Review Guarantee: your log and the screenshots you send us
  • messages you send us by email.

We do not receive or store your full card details. Card payments are handled by Square.

What we do with your information

We use your information to:

  • answer your enquiries and prepare quotes
  • process, programme, deliver and support your order
  • run your account, if you have one, and show you your orders
  • create and, only once you have approved it, publish your digital profile
  • check and pay guarantee claims and handle returns and refunds
  • keep the business records the law requires.

Our legal reasons. We use your information to carry out your order or to take steps you ask for before ordering (contract); to keep records required by law, such as tax records (legal obligation); and, in a limited way, to run and protect our business, for example handling a complaint (legitimate interests).

No marketing without your agreement. We do not send marketing emails unless you have told us you want them. We do not sell your information.

Published profiles. A digital profile you approve will be visible on the public internet. We never publish your delivery address because you placed an order.

Who we share it with

We only share your information where we need to, with:

  • our database provider, which stores our accounts, orders and Studio submissions in a private database (servers in Ireland, EU)
  • Square, which processes card payments
  • our website hosting provider
  • our email provider, to send and receive emails
  • the delivery company carrying your order (name and delivery address only).

We will also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Orders: we keep your order details (what you bought, the price and the date) for as long as UK tax law requires, at least 5 years. We never keep your card details.
  • Accounts and daily review logs: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to an order: 12 months.
  • Studio submissions that are not used: 12 months.
  • Guarantee claims: with the order records above.

After that, we delete the information securely.

Security

We take sensible steps to keep your information safe. Our database is private, account passwords are stored in protected (hashed) form, Studio photos are kept in private storage, and each customer can only see their own account data. Information sent between your browser and our website is encrypted.

Cookies and browser storage

A cookie is a small file a website saves on your device. Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track which pages you visit.

To make the site work, we save a few small items in your own browser's storage, such as your basket, your theme choice and your daily review log. These stay on your device and are not sent to us unless you choose to submit something or save your log to your account. If you sign in, a sign-in token is kept in your browser so you stay signed in. You can clear all of this at any time in your browser settings; the basket and saved log will then be lost.

If we ever want to add tracking or analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Links to other websites

Our website and cards link to other websites, such as Google review pages. Once you leave our website, we have no control over those sites and this policy does not apply to them. Please read their own privacy policies.

Controlling your personal information

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • delete your information
  • limit or stop how we use it
  • send your information to you or another organisation in a usable format.

You can also object to how we use your information. These requests are free. Email hello@nuvotap.com and we will reply within one month.

If you have an account, you can update your details there at any time. To delete your account, email hello@nuvotap.com from the address on the account.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
