Returns & Refunds Policy

Updated 15 October 2026 · Standard UK statutory consumer rights; no additional voluntary 30-day return scheme

Changed your mind about standard goods?

For most eligible consumer purchases made at a distance, you can tell us you wish to cancel within 14 days of receiving the goods. After notifying us, you generally have a further 14 days to send the goods back. Contact hello@nuvotap.com with your order details and cancellation request.

Who pays return postage?

For eligible change-of-mind returns, the buyer is responsible for paying the cost of return postage. We recommend a tracked service and will give you the return address when you contact us. This is disclosed before you order. This rule does not override your statutory rights where goods are faulty, not as described, or incorrectly supplied: in those circumstances NUVOTAP covers reasonable return costs where required by law.

When do you receive your refund?

For eligible cancellations, we refund the goods price and the cost of our least expensive standard outbound delivery option where applicable. We normally issue the refund within 14 days after receiving returned goods or sufficient evidence that they were sent back, whichever happens first. We may make a lawful deduction for handling beyond what is necessary to assess goods, where applicable. The refund is made using the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise.

Faulty or incorrect goods

If a card is defective, damaged when supplied, not as described, or we supplied the wrong product, contact us. The Consumer Rights Act 2015 provides statutory remedies, including a short-term right to reject qualifying faulty goods within 30 days, and potential repair, replacement or further remedies beyond that period. We will correct our own programming and fulfilment mistakes without charging you.

Personalised goods and digital services

Some genuinely made-to-order personalised goods may be excluded from change-of-mind cancellation rules, but standard cards are not automatically excluded merely because a URL was programmed. Digital profile creation and hosting may be subject to different cancellation rules for services or digital content. Any applicable exception or charge for starting work during the cancellation period will be explained and required consent obtained before work begins. Faulty-goods and other statutory rights are never removed by this paragraph.

How to request a return

Email hello@nuvotap.com with your name, purchase details, item and reason (you do not need to provide a reason for an eligible change-of-mind cancellation). Please wait for a confirmed return address before posting the item.

This policy reflects our intended approach under UK law and does not restrict rights provided by law. Read more: GOV.UK consumer returns.
